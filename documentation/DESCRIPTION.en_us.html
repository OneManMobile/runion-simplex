Runion Simplex is Runion Neo's dot system made for the terminal. Every glyph is made of straight lines from dot to dot on a grid of 3 by 7, exactly as in Runion Neo, but lowercase is drawn on a shorter lattice so it stands lower than its capitals, and the monospaced cell is a little wider.

It covers Latin and the whole Unicode Runic block, in every weight from Light to Bold. The typeface is defined in one text file of dot-to-dot connections. To contribute, see github.com/OneManMobile/runion-simplex.
