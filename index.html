Runion Simplex

Runion Neo's dot system, made for terminals. Every glyph — Latin and runes alike — is straight lines between dots on a 3 × 7 grid; lowercase sits on a shorter lattice so text reads like code. One square pen per weight, from Light to Bold.

The grid — click dots to draw

  

    
    

      
Pick any glyph below to load it, or draw your own: click a dot to start a line, keep clicking to continue it. Click the last dot again to end the stroke (that also makes a lone dot).

      
A point is written xy — column, then row. 06 is top-left, 20 bottom-right; a and b are the rows below the baseline, 7 and 8 the accent rows above. Lowercase is drawn on this same lattice and set shorter by the build; the dashed guides show baseline, x-height and cap height. Lines join any two points with a dash. That string is the entire definition of a glyph. copy gives the whole line for glyphs.txt: name, character, strokes.
